About the centre

Legal education that answers to practice, not to theory

The centre was founded to close the distance between legal qualification and legal competence. Every programme is built around the files a practitioner actually carries — pleadings, contracts, arbitration bundles, compliance reviews — and is taught by people who carry them for a living.

Certificates issued by the centre are recorded in a public register. Any employer, court or bar association can confirm one in seconds using the verifier on this page.
